Why Krishna Dudh Dairy

We didn't build a brand. We kept a route.

Krishna started with two cans of milk on a bicycle in Kathmandu. The route is longer now — covering wards across the valley — but the promise hasn't moved: nothing leaves the collection point until it's tested, and nothing sits in a warehouse overnight.

  • No added water, no preservatives — what leaves the cow is what reaches your door.
  • Cold chain the whole way — collection, transport and last-mile delivery stay under 4°C.
  • Same-day complaints, same-day fixes — message us on WhatsApp and a person replies, not a bot.

Every single morning

The route your milk actually takes

Four checkpoints, three hours, one cold chain. This is what happens before your bell rings.

🥛
  1. 🐄

    Collection

    Milk drawn from partner farms across farms in the Kathmandu Valley, tested for fat and purity on the spot.

  2. ❄️

    Chilling

    Cooled to below 4°C within the hour at our Kathmandu collection point — no delay, no room-temperature wait.

  3. 📦

    Packing

    Sealed into hygienic pouches and crates for milk; ghee, paneer and curd are batched from the previous day's stock.

  4. 🛵

    Delivery

    Riders cover Kathmandu, Bhaktapur & Lalitpur on fixed rounds — you can track your rider's rough slot.
